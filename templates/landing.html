Concurrency Mechanics

Pessimistic Row-Level Locking

How Apex Bank protects against double-spending and simultaneous debit race conditions.

SELECT ... FOR UPDATE

When an account initiates a transfer, its database row is locked at the storage engine level. Concurrent transfers against the same account wait until the first lock commits.

CHECK Constraints & Triggers

Table-level CHECK (balance >= 0) and MySQL triggers ensure that even if application logic were bypassed, the database engine forbids negative balances.

Deterministic Lock Ordering

Transfer locks are always acquired in alphabetical order of account numbers (e.g. Account A before Account B), mathematically eliminating deadlock cycles.
